Progress grid: three directions at 1140

Same 30-student Biology class in all three (Stages 1–3 due, 4–6 not due; today is 25 Jan 2027). The three differ on where the boldness sits and how the checkpoint text is reached. All three are live: sign off, revoke, undo, re-sort and Hide names work, and share one state, so a change in one shows in the others.

Common to all: one click signs off and the cell changes in place; rows never move until the teacher presses Re-sort; revoke confirms in a strip under the row; Hide names blurs names and dates for the projector. Student names and stage dates are sample content.
