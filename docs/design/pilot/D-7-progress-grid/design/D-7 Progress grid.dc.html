Pack D-7 · progress grid and sign-offs · direction 1b Bands on 2e Navy

Progress tab, sign-offs, and the stage card

The memorable thing is the behind-by band heading: a large amber numeral over each group of students. A stage picker above the grid switches between all checkpoints and one checkpoint at a time (?stage=3), where the full checkpoint text heads a single wide column of large Sign off buttons. Every frame is live: sign off, undo, revoke, re-sort and Hide names all work.

Sample content: student names, stage dates and "today" (25 Jan 2027) are placeholders. Checkpoint text is quoted from V2__science_templates.sql and V3__business_template.sql; short column labels are each checkpoint's opening words. The grid has no primary (filled) button: Sign off is a secondary control repeated per cell.

1 and 2 · The grid, mid-year

Biology, 30 students, Stages 1–3 due. At 390 the picker opens on the latest due stage; All lists every student with what's missing.

1b · One checkpoint at a time

The picker or a column heading opens ?stage=3. Same bands, same order, full checkpoint text in the header, 44px Sign off buttons.

3 · Signing off: before, during, after

One click. The cell turns busy in place, then reads "Signed off today" with Undo. Rows and bands stay put; Re-sort appears with a count.

4 · Revoking

A signed-off cell opens a confirm strip under its row, naming the checkpoint in full. Afterwards the cell is Due again; the revoked sign-off stays in the record.

5 · A failed sign-off

D-1's compact alert, under the row it belongs to. The cell is unchanged and still offers Sign off; Try again repeats the request.

6 and 7 · Nothing due yet; no stage dates

Both keep the grid, because early sign-offs are allowed. A plain sentence, no alarm colour. With no dates, a link to the Component tab.

8 and 9 · No component; no approved students

D-6's empty card: what the space is for and the one next step.

10 · Everyone up to date

A specific sentence and the next date. No numeral, no amber, no celebration.

11 · Business: Stage 6 with no checkpoint, and the report

The table has six checkpoint columns. Stage 6 stays in the picker as a dashed "No checkpoint" item, so the sequence reads 1–6 then Report, but it adds no empty column.

12 · Failed load

The shared error panel from D-1, in place of the grid. Tabs stay usable.

13 · Projected: Hide names on

One switch beside the heading, always in the same place. It blurs names, dates and log lines; the bands, counts and cell states stay readable. Off by default, remembered per device.

14 · Student view: Checkpoints above the log

The teacher arrives here from the grid to act, so checkpoints come first. The same Sign off and Revoke as the grid. A revoked sign-off shows who revoked it and when.

15 · Student stage card (D-3): the checkpoint box, signed off

Same card, one more state for its checkpoint box. Green ground and edge, the words "Signed off", and the date as a sentence. Shown beside the two existing states.
