What you see today

  • Stage 5 at 8 Jan looks exactly like a saved date. Nothing says it isn't saved yet.
  • Stages are round dots with a number on a stem; the drawer draws them as S5 squares. Two looks for one thing.
  • Today is a grey dashed line.
  • Show calendar opens a read-only month grid with its own arrows: a second calendar on the same page as the drawer's.

What option 1 changes

  • Stage 5 is a dashed S5 square, and the key says Not saved yet, the same as in the drawer.
  • Stages are S1–S6 squares and items are lettered rings, in both places. A square is a stage and a ring is an item, by shape, not just by fill.
  • Today is the gold disc, in both places.
  • The gap labels stay (they fit at page width), and the month you're in stays shaded.
  • Show calendar and its month grid go. The drawer is the calendar now.
  • Still read-only: it never sets a date. One component draws both lines, so the next change to a mark happens once.
