Teacher component setup

Every state in the pack spec, at 1140 and 390. The stage-dates editor is direction 3a: compact numbered rows under a collapsible year view, batch Save dates, checkpoints read-only. Rough explorations are kept in D-5 Explorations.dc.html.

Frames marked page content start at the tab row — the app header above it is D-1's and identical on every frame; states 1 and 3 show it in full. Placeholder dates throughout; stage names, hours and completion dates are real.

The three rules the page carries

Numbers are the SEC's, letters are yours. Stages are numbered and can't be renamed, added or removed; teacher items are lettered in date order. The mark shape says the same thing: filled circle for a stage, hollow for an item, navy end-stop for the completion date.

Red refuses, amber asks. Red is only for a date the server rejected. Out-of-order dates and a moved completion date are amber — 2px edge and tint, never a solid fill. Solid amber stays what it was in D-2: a count of pending work.

Nothing was lost. One Save dates for all six stages, a changed-count beside it, and a confirmation before leaving with unsaved dates. Items save one at a time, as their endpoints do.
