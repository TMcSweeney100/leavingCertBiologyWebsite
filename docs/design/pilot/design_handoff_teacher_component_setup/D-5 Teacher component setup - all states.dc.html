Teacher component setup

The memorable element is the year line: the class's whole coursework year on one rule, with the SEC completion date as an end-stop nothing may pass. Under it, a month calendar the teacher can open or leave shut. Everything else is D-1's field group, D-2's cards and hairlines, and one navy primary.

One rule carries the whole page: a number is a national stage, a letter is something the teacher added. Numbers are filled navy, letters are hollow. It holds on the line, in the calendar, in the key and in the rows.

No new tokens. The two warnings use D-1's amber message, whose precedent is the rate-limit notice on /login — amber already means "read this, nothing is broken", and pending work is one instance of that, not the whole meaning. Red stays for the refused date. Reasoning and exact copy in NOTES.md.
